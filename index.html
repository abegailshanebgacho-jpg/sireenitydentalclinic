Our Services

Orthodontics

  • Orthodontics focuses on correcting misaligned teeth and bite issues using braces to improve oral health, function, and appearance.
    Types of Orthodontic Treatment
    • Metal Braces
    • Ceramic Braces
    • Retainers (Post-treatment)

    Benifits
    • Straighter, more confident smile
    • Improved bite and chewing function
    • Easier oral hygiene
    • Prevents future dental problems

Full Oral & X-ray

  • A comprehensive dental examination combined with digital X-rays to assess your overall oral health and detect problems early.
    • Visual examination of teeth, gums, and bite
    • Digital dental X-rays
    • Gum health assessment
    • Review of dental and medical history

    Benifits
    • Detects cavities, infections, and bone loss early
    • Helps prevent costly future treatments
    • Ensures accurate diagnosis and treatment planning
    • Supports long-term oral health

Dental Cleaning

  • Professional dental cleaning removes plaque, tartar, and stains to keep your teeth and gums healthy and your smile fresh.
    Includes
    • Removal of plaque and tartar
    • Polishing to remove surface stains
    • Gum health assessment
    • Gentle flossing and rinsing
    • Oral hygiene advice from the dentist

    Why Dental Cleaning Is Important
    • Helps prevent cavities and gum disease
    • Reduces bad breath
    • Helps prevent costly future treatments
    • Keeps teeth bright and healthy

Fillings, Rootcanal

  • Fillings and root canal treatment are restorative procedures used to repair damaged or infected teeth, relieve pain, and restore normal function.
    Includes
    • Thorough dental examination
    • Local anesthesia for comfort
    • Removal of decay or infection
    • Tooth restoration (filling or crown recommendation)
    • Post-treatment care instructions

    Benifits
    • Relieves tooth pain and sensitivity
    • Stops infection from spreading
    • Saves the natural tooth
    • Restores chewing and appearance

Extractions

  • Tooth extraction is the safe removal of a damaged, decayed, or problematic tooth to relieve pain and protect surrounding teeth and gums.
    Types Of Extractions
    • Simple extraction
    • Surgical extraction
    • Wisdom tooth removal

    Included
    • Pre-extraction assessment and X-ray
    • Local anesthesia for comfort
    • Safe and gentle tooth removal
    • Post-extraction care instructions
    • Prescription (if needed)

    Recovery & Aftercare
    • Mild swelling or soreness is normal
    • Avoid hard foods for 24–48 hours
    • Follow aftercare instructions to prevent infection
    • Healing typically takes 7–10 days

    Benifits
    • Relieves pain and infection
    • Prevents damage to nearby teeth
    • Improves overall oral health
    • repares mouth for further treatment if needed

Full Partial Dentures

  • Full and partial dentures are custom-made removable appliances designed to replace missing teeth, restore function, and improve smile appearance.
    Types Of Dentures
    • Full Dentures – Replace all missing teeth in the upper or lower jaw
    • Partial Dentures – Replace one or more missing teeth while preserving remaining natural teeth

    Includes
    • Oral examination and impressions
    • Custom denture design and fitting
    • Bite and comfort adjustments
    • Instructions on care and maintenance
    • Follow-up adjustments if needed
    Benifits
    • Restores chewing and speech
    • Improves facial structure and confidence
    • Comfortable, natural-looking smile
    • Affordable tooth replacement option

Dental Cleaning

  • Crowns and bridges are fixed dental restorations used to repair damaged teeth or replace missing teeth, restoring strength, function, and appearance.
    Difference Between Crowns & Bridges
    • Dental Crowns – Cover and protect a weakened or damaged tooth
    • Dental Bridges – Replace one or more missing teeth by anchoring to nearby teeth

    Includes
    • Oral examination and treatment planning
    • Tooth preparation and impressions
    • Temporary crown or bridge (if needed)
    • Custom-made permanent restoration
    • Final fitting and bite adjustment

    Benifits
    • Restores chewing strength
    • Protects weakened teeth
    • Improves smile appearance
    • Prevents shifting of nearby teeth
    • Long-lasting and natural-looking

Veneer and Bleaching

  • Veneers and teeth bleaching are cosmetic dental treatments designed to enhance the color, shape, and overall appearance of your smile.
    Difference Between Veneers & Bleaching
    • Veneers – Thin, custom-made shells that cover the front surface of teeth to improve shape, color, and alignment
    • Teeth Bleaching – Professional whitening treatment to remove stains and brighten natural teeth

    Includes
    • Smile assessment and consultation
    • Shade analysis and treatment planning
    • Safe, professional bleaching or veneer preparation
    • Custom veneer fabrication (for veneers)
    • Post-treatment care instructions

    Benifits
    • Whiter, brighter smile
    • Improved tooth shape and symmetry
    • Natural-looking, long-lasting results
    • Boosts confidence and self-esteem

Dental Implants

  • Dental implants are permanent tooth replacements that look, feel, and function like natural teeth by anchoring securely into the jawbone.
    Includes
    • Comprehensive oral examination and X-ray
    • Implant treatment planning
    • Surgical placement of implant
    • Healing and integration period
    • Final crown or prosthetic placement
    Benifits
    • Looks and feels like a natural tooth
    • Prevents bone loss in the jaw
    • Long-lasting and durable
    • Improves chewing, speech, and confidence
    • No damage to nearby teeth

Dental Certificate

  • A dental certificate is an official document issued by a licensed dentist confirming a patient’s oral health status for school, employment, travel, or medical requirements.
    Purpose of a Dental Certificate
    • School or university requirements
    • Employment or pre-employment clearance
    • Travel or visa applications
    • Medical or surgical clearance
    • Insurance or legal documentation

    Includes
    • Oral examination by a licensed dentist
    • Assessment of teeth and gum condition
    • Necessary dental X-rays (if required)
    • Official signed and stamped certificate
